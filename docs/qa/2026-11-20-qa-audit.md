- **Evidence:** throwaway scripts ran the real `lib/` modules (pure functions, run with `npx tsx`). The shape editor was also driven in headless Chrome. React wiring was traced line by line.
- **Re-verified by lead:** the headline findings were checked a second time. They are marked **✔ re-verified** below, either by reading the cited lines or by running an independent script.
- **Verdicts:**
  - **CONFIRMED** = reproduced by a script, a screenshot, or a code path with no ambiguity.
  - **LIKELY** = strong evidence, not executed.
- **Severities:**
  - **High** = data loss or wrong data, or a core action that does not work.
  - **Medium** = wrong result with a workaround.
  - **Low** = cosmetic or an edge case.
- **Scripts:** the evidence scripts are in the session scratchpad (`…/scratchpad/qa/<area>/`), which is temporary. Their key output is quoted here.

## 2. Stage fill and stage outline editing (ST)

### ST-1 · High · CONFIRMED ✔ re-verified — a stage touching a wall is judged as "crossing" it
- **Where:** [wall-clip.ts:41-85](../../lib/studio/wall-clip.ts#L41-L85), used by `take()` in [canvas-stage.tsx:5306-5317](../../app/(app)/studio/canvas-stage.tsx#L5306-L5317).
- **Repro:** in Amare hall 1, draw a stage flush on the west wall, which drifts 5 mm over 14 m. Type a new length on any side. The box goes red, and every grip drag is refused too.
- **Second case:** a stage whose side has another wall *ending* on it, i.e. a T-junction such as an outside wall meeting the hall wall. It is refused in the same way.
- **Cause:** two flaws.
  - The "touching is not crossing" tolerance (2 mm) is measured *along the stage's side* (`t*len`), not across the wall. When a near-parallel wall line passes between the side's two corners, which are rounded to the mm, the crossing lands hundreds of mm along the side, so it counts.
  - `segmentCrossing` counts the wall's own end points as hits (`u ∈ [0,1]`).
  - On top of both, `take()` re-tests the *whole* outline. Once an outline is judged to cross, every later edit is red.
- **Lead's independent repro:** a 4×6 stage whose west side lies on that drifting wall gives `crossesWalls = true`. A box with a wall ending on its side also gives `true`.
- **The same flaw cuts drawing:**
  - A side drawn along the west wall is clipped after 0.7–1.3 m.
  - Along the 50° wall in hall 2, 12 of 30 starts are clipped.
  - Some clips leave a side under 10 cm, which `take()` then refuses forever.
- **Fix:** judge crossing by perpendicular penetration beyond the tolerance. Count a wall end on a side only when the wall body enters the outline. Test only what the edit changed.

### ST-2 · Medium · CONFIRMED — which end of a typed side moves depends on the order you clicked the corners
- **Where:** [canvas-stage.tsx:5345](../../app/(app)/studio/canvas-stage.tsx#L5345).
- **Repro:** in a square room, put a 6×4 stage against the north wall, 50 cm from the east wall. Type its front as 700.
  - Drawn west→east: accepted (it grows west).
  - Drawn east→west: red (it grows into the east wall).
- **Cause:** when no neighbouring side hugs a wall, `keep` defaults to `"start"`, which comes from the polygon's winding. The other end is never tried.
- **Fix:** if the kept end fails, try the other end, or keep the end nearer a wall.

### ST-3 · Medium · CONFIRMED — some resize paths push a stage through a wall, and every later typed figure is red
- **Where:** [studio-screen.tsx:2934](../../app/(app)/studio/studio-screen.tsx#L2934) (`resizeStageArea` → `resizeRect`, which grows about the front's middle), [studio-screen.tsx:3200](../../app/(app)/studio/studio-screen.tsx#L3200) (the bar's typed מידות), body drags, resize handles.

- **Fix:** route these paths through `crossesWalls`/`clipToWalls`, or anchor the grow on the wall side.

### ST-4 · Medium · CONFIRMED — a refused figure never says why, and the pill shows metres while the box takes centimetres
- **Where:** [canvas-stage.tsx:5366-5368](../../app/(app)/studio/canvas-stage.tsx#L5366-L5368) (only `refused: true`), pill text ~:5447 (`"6.00"`, no unit), box ~:5454 (`600` ס״מ).

### ST-5 · Medium · CONFIRMED — a deck pinned or a hole made outside the outline is kept, counted and billed on a NEW stage

### ST-9 · Feature request (U2) — suggest the area a drawing closes with the walls
- **Today:**

  - It knows nothing about rooms or faces.
  - It always closes with a straight chord back to the first corner.
  - `detectFaces`/`faceAt` ([lib/venues/faces.ts](../../lib/venues/faces.ts)) exist, but only /halls, outputs and `eventPlan` use them.

  - Preview it dashed; Enter or a click accepts, and Tab flips to the other side.

- **Prerequisite:** ST-1. Today an outline that follows the walls exactly is refused at a T-junction.

## 3. Magnet and snapping (MG)

"The magnet" is two different mechanisms in the code:
- **(A)** the **הצמדה** command (Magnet icon, the ⋯ menu of the multi-select and stage-group bars). It runs `stickStages` → `stickBoxes` and is for **stages only**.
- **(B)** the **drag-time hold** (`nudgeApart`) plus the 6 px **snap** (`ctx.snap`).

Both leave gaps.

### MG-1 · High · CONFIRMED — הצמדה throws a piece out past a non-rectangular stage (3 m gap)
- **Where:** [studio-screen.tsx:3978-4011](../../app/(app)/studio/studio-screen.tsx#L3978-L4011) (`stickItems`/`stickStages`), [stage-draw.ts:221-283](../../lib/studio/stage-draw.ts#L221-L283) (`stickBoxes`).
- **Repro:** a 6×3 stage merged with a 1×3 m catwalk (a T), and a 244×122 deck already flush on the main front beside the catwalk → select both → ⋯ → הצמדה. The deck jumps out past the catwalk tip, leaving a **3000 mm gap**.
- **Same problem:** anything in an L/U notch, beside an apron, or next to a half-round.
- **Cause:** every stage is boxed by its outline's min/max. The deck "overlaps" the T's bounding box, so it is pushed to the box's far edge.
- **Fix:** stick on the real outlines (the polygon path `pushTogether` already uses for shaped stages), and leave alone anything that already shares rim.

### MG-2 · High · CONFIRMED — Ctrl+G: a deck flush in a notch is grouped instead of merged, or merged in the wrong place
- **Where:** stage.ts:2746-2752 (`squareStage`), 2856-2890 (`mergeTouching` → `pushTogether` → `stickBoxes`).
- **Repro:** an L stage (6×4 with a 2×2 bite) plus a 2×2 deck flush in the bite → Ctrl+G. The two are **grouped, not merged**.
  - U stage: the deck is moved to the *other side* of the U and merged there. The notch stays open and the stage grows 2 m.
  - The T from MG-1: grouped, not merged.
- **Cause:** an L/T/U has only square corners, so it takes the bounding-box path (same defect as MG-1).
- **Fix:** use the box path only when every part is a plain rectangle.

### MG-3 · High · CONFIRMED ✔ re-verified — a slow or corrective drag of ONE item never snaps or holds
- **Where:** [canvas-stage.tsx:1569-1571](../../app/(app)/studio/canvas-stage.tsx#L1569-L1571). The group path at :1545 is correct.
- **Repro:** drag a stage briskly so it ends ~3 px short of its neighbour. Then drag it slowly to close the gap. No guides appear and nothing snaps; the gap stays.
- **Cause:** the 10 px "travelled" latch compares the pointer with `self`, the item's box taken from the **live** document. That box is rebuilt from this same drag's previous frame. So the question asked is "did this *frame* move 10 px?", not "has the drag moved 10 px since it began?". It trips only on a single frame of ≥10 px: about 600 px/s at 60 Hz, never at 1000 px/s on a 144 Hz screen.
- **Fix:** measure from the gesture's origin box (captured on the first frame, as `solids.origin`/`groupDrag.start` already are).

### MG-4 · Medium–High · CONFIRMED — stages at an angle snap by their axis-aligned box, leaving a 232–1000 mm gap that Ctrl+G then won't merge
- **Where:** canvas-stage.tsx:748-765, 3703-3708 (`turnedExtent`); geometry.ts:634 (`rotatedExtent`, whose own comment admits "stop a little apart"); `lib/studio/snap.ts` (world-axis references only).
- **Repro:** two 4×2 stages along a wall at 30° → drag one beside the other until it snaps. The real gap is **232 mm** at 15°/30° and **1000 mm** at 45°.
  - A rail drop at the true flush spot is pushed away to an 866 mm gap.
  - Ctrl+G then groups them instead of merging (they share under 300 mm of side).
- **Fix:** when mover and neighbour share a rotation, snap and collide in their common frame (oriented boxes, or a separating-axis test).

### MG-5 · Medium · CONFIRMED — the magnet only pushes apart; it never pulls things together
- **Where:** [collide.ts:97-100](../../lib/studio/collide.ts#L97-L100) (overlap only); snap.ts `align()` (the nearest reference wins).
- **Repro:** drag a stage to 30–55 mm short of its neighbour while an unrelated edge (a table, a wall corner) lines up within 6 px. It snaps to the unrelated line, and a 40 mm gap stays.
- **Fix:** give "flush to a neighbour of the same kind" priority over other references, or add real attraction within the reach.

### MG-6 · Medium · CONFIRMED — a stage with a carpet laid over it snaps to its own carpet
- **Where:** canvas-stage.tsx:1391-1404 (`snapBoxesFor` excludes the cargo, but not carpets whose `cover` points at the cargo); actions.ts:220 (`settleCovers` re-lays the carpet every frame).
- **Repro:** "שטיח על הבמה" → drag the stage. It lurches in 50 mm steps and stops 37 mm short of a neighbour.
- **Fix:** in `snapBoxesFor`, drop placements whose `cover.stageId`/`featureId` is in the cargo.

### MG-7 · Medium · CONFIRMED — הצמדה does nothing when the largest stage isn't square to the others, or when a stage is ≥0.5° off
- **Where:** stage-draw.ts:223-231 (the anchor is the largest stage; squareness is checked against it only).
- **Repro:** a big stage at 30° plus two 0° decks 200 mm apart → הצמדה. Nothing moves, and the hint "הבמות כבר צמודות — או שהן בזוויות שונות" appears. When some stages are skipped while others move, the skipped ones are never mentioned.

### MG-8 · Medium · CONFIRMED ✔ re-verified — a table dropped from the rail onto another table is never pushed off it

- **Repro:** drag a table off the rail and let go half on top of another. It lands overlapping.
- **Fix:** add `tables: "table"` to `SOLID_BY_CATEGORY`.

### MG-9 · Low–Medium · CONFIRMED — round and rotated tables are held apart by an invisible box
- **Repro:** a 1.8 m round table dragged diagonally toward another is held with ~746 mm of air between the rims. Two 45° tables with 300 mm of real air are pushed **1485 mm** apart on drop.
- **Fix:** collide circles as circles, and turned shapes by their outline.

### MG-10 · Low–Medium · LIKELY — a flight on the side where two stages meet is kept across the neighbour's deck

### MG-12 · Low · CONFIRMED — a pair that overlapped when the drag began is never held again, even once dragged clear
- **Where:** collide.ts:102-117, frozen in `beginSolids` (canvas-stage.tsx:1460-1464). This contradicts the comment at collide.ts:165.

### MG-13 · Low · CONFIRMED — /halls: in a group drag only the pressed feature snaps; a round feature turned 45° snaps as a 4.24 m box
- **Where:** [halls-screen.tsx:713-724, 833-859](../../app/(app)/halls/halls-screen.tsx#L713-L724); `featureBox` :249-254 has no circle exception.

### MG-14 · Low · CONFIRMED — click-to-place lines floor items up with chandeliers

**Feature gap:** the hold is same-kind by design: a table never holds off a stage, a bar, a sofa, a column or a wall. Bringing more kinds in needs a product decision on which pairs **may** overlap. A chuppah on a stage, a rug under a table and items on a banquette must stay allowed.

## 4. Banquette and sofa seating (BQ)

### BQ-1 · High · CONFIRMED ✔ re-verified — U4: the stage-edge banquette's sofa fields always read 0, and tabbing through them rewrites the sofa
- **Where:**
  - [studio-screen.tsx:3157-3158](../../app/(app)/studio/studio-screen.tsx#L3157-L3158): the edge rows are built as `{ kind, seats, set }` only, with no `widthMm`/`depthMm`.
  - [inspector.tsx:1614-1627](../../app/(app)/studio/inspector.tsx#L1614-L1627): the edge's `BenchSidesFields` gets no `lengthMm`/`depthMm`.
  - [inspector.tsx:3107-3110](../../app/(app)/studio/inspector.tsx#L3107-L3110): the fields read `row.widthMm ?? lengthMm ?? 0` and `row.depthMm ?? 0`, so both show **0**.
- **Repro:**
  1. Select a stage → its banquette.
  2. Set צד האולם (or צד הבמה) to ספה.
  3. Look at רוחב הספה and עומק המושב: both show 0, whatever was typed or laid.
  4. Tab through them without typing.
  - Result: on blur, `NumberField` clamps the 0 up to its minimum (60 cm / 20 cm) and **commits** it. The sofa shrinks to a 60 cm run (1 seat) and a 20 cm seat.
  - Typed values seem to stick until the panel remounts, then read 0 again.
  - The "לכל האורך" button and the "מתוך X של הבנקט" note never appear.
- **Fix:**
  - Spread the layout's `shape.bench.back/front` (with `widthMm`/`depthMm`) into the edge rows.
  - Pass `lengthMm`/`depthMm` to `BenchSidesFields`.
  - Separately, make `NumberField` not commit on a blur with no edit.

### BQ-2 · Medium · CONFIRMED — U4: the sofa depth shown, drawn and stored drift apart, and nothing stops two sides overlapping
- **Where:** [bench.ts:136-164](../../lib/design-document/bench.ts#L136-L164); inspector.tsx:3110 (min 20, max 150).
- **Cases:**
  - **(a) Default sofa on a 45 cm banquette:** the default seat (14.5 cm) is below the field's minimum of 20. The field shows 15; a blur writes 20.
  - **(b) Seat deeper than the banquette:** type 80 on a 45 cm banquette. The field shows 45, the plan draws 43, and 80 is stored. Deepening the banquette to 1 m later brings a hidden 80 cm seat back.
  - **(c) Sofa run longer than the banquette:** a typed 400 cm run on a 2.4 m banquette shows 240. Stretching the banquette to 6 m gives a 400 cm sofa, not the whole length.
  - **(d) Two sides:** each may be up to `depth − 20` deep, with no shared limit. Two 40 cm seats on a 60 cm strip overlap by 200 mm. A chairs side plus a 50 cm sofa puts cushions over the chairs' table half.
- **Fix:**
  - Clamp when writing (to depth − the other side's seat − the band).
  - Show what is drawn.
  - Base the field's minimum on the default.

### U5 "changing a side affects the other" — what is intended and what is not

**Intended (keep):**
- A box's edge grip holds the opposite edge; a corner grip holds the opposite corner; Shift locks the ratio.
- A circle scales about its centre.
- Derived shapes (trapezoid, hexagon, octagon, stadium, crescent, horseshoe, ח) are parametric from W×D, so a new width moves every side. For example, a hexagon's top is always W/2.
- A free-form drawing given a typed W/D rescales as a whole, bows included.
- Bands (hollow, ring, arc) keep their depth in mm.
- A rectangle or L given a typed side keeps its right angles.

**Annoying** (SH-5 … SH-9): today "only this side" exists only as the box's edge grips and the per-side length lock (נעילת האורך). The point editor has no choice of which end moves, and no Shift-to-keep-square like the stage tool's.
